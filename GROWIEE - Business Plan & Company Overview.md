# **GROWIEE \- BUSINESS PLAN & COMPANY OVERVIEW**

# **1\. Executive Summary**

GROWIEE is a Direct-to-Consumer (D2C) baby apparel brand launching in Sri Lanka, designed specifically to serve the high-demand 0 to 9-month infant segment. Built on the core ethos of safety, supreme comfort, and thoughtful engineering, GROWIEE provides ultra-soft, breathable, and non-toxic 100% cotton baby clothing sets.

By standardizing our offering into high-value **3-Piece Apparel Packs (3-Kits)**, GROWIEE simplifies buying decisions for expecting mothers and gift shoppers while optimizing manufacturing and fulfillment efficiencies for high scalability.

## **Brand Key Facts**

* **Brand Name:** GROWIEE  
* **Domain:** growiee.com  
* **Industry:** Infant & Baby Apparel (0–9 Months)  
* **Primary Market:** Sri Lanka (Direct-to-Consumer, Social Commerce, and E-Commerce)  
* **Core Product Offering:** Premium 3-piece apparel sets / 3-kit packs  
* **Mission Statement:** To engineer safe, non-toxic, ultra-soft apparel that adapts to an infant’s rapid growth during their first 9 months, offering parents absolute peace of mind and accessible luxury.

# **2\. Market Analysis & Industry Overview**

## **2.1 Domestic Retail Landscape in Sri Lanka**

The Sri Lankan domestic apparel retail market represents a multi-billion LKR ecosystem. In recent years, consumer shopping habits have shifted rapidly toward social-commerce platforms (Facebook, Instagram, and WhatsApp) paired with islandwide Cash-on-Delivery (COD) services.

Despite economic shifts, essential newborn spending remains resilient. Parents and relatives consistently prioritize infant comfort and skin safety, keeping demand stable throughout the year.

## **2.2 Target Sizing Segment**

GROWIEE focuses exclusively on the critical early lifecycle stage: **0 to 9 months**.

* **Newborn (NB):** Specialized hospital bag essentials and initial coming-home wear.  
* **0–3 Months:** Rapid growth phase requiring frequent outfit changes.  
* **3–6 Months:** Increased movement and sensory exploration requiring high stretch and seam strength.  
* **6–9 Months:** Early crawling phase demanding extra durability and breathable thermoregulation.

Focusing strictly on 0–9 months maximizes inventory turnover, simplifies supply chain logistics, and concentrates marketing acquisition costs on a clear demographic target: expecting mothers and newborn gift shoppers.

## **2.3 Competitive Positioning**

The Sri Lankan baby apparel market is currently divided into two main segments:

1. **Low-Cost Synthetic/Blended Imports:** Inexpensive, but often made with harsh synthetic fibers, stiff seams, and untested chemical dyes that can irritate delicate infant skin.  
2. **High-End Imported Boutique Brands:** High quality, but sold at prohibitive markups with limited local supply consistency.

**GROWIEE’s Advantage:** GROWIEE bridges this gap by offering locally produced, export-grade 100% combed/interlock cotton apparel at an accessible price point, providing premium quality without the high boutique cost.

# **3\. Product Portfolio & Sourcing Strategy**

## **3.1 Product Offerings & Configuration**

GROWIEE standardizes its inventory around the **3-Kit Pack Configuration**. Each bundle is curated to offer complementary essentials (e.g., bodysuits, sleepsuits, or gentle pants) packaged in uniform, brand-aligned bundles.

| Age Category | Primary Focus & Feature | Key Bundle Components |
| :---- | :---- | :---- |
| **Newborn (NB)** | Delicacy & Easy Access | 3x Ultra-Soft Onesies with Scratch Mitt Fold-overs |
| **0–3 Months** | Daily Essentials & Comfort | 3x Breathable Everyday Interlock Bodysuits |
| **3–6 Months** | Active Stretch & Mobility | 3x Comfort-Fit Grow-With-Me Rompers |
| **6–9 Months** | Durable Softness | 3x Heavy-Duty Interlock Play Sets / Sleepsuits |

## **3.2 Material & Safety Standards**

Baby skin is significantly thinner and more sensitive than adult skin. GROWIEE enforces strict product safety and material standards:

* **100% Interlock / Combed Cotton:** High-grade natural cotton fibers ensuring softness, breathability, and natural temperature control.  
* **Non-Toxic AZO-Free Dyes:** Safe, hypoallergenic dyes that prevent contact dermatitis and chemical irritation.  
* **Nickel-Free Snaps:** Anti-allergenic hardware engineered to protect sensitive skin from friction and reactions.  
* **Envelope Necks & Flat Seams:** Expandable necklines for quick, stress-free dressing, combined with smooth, flat-lock stitching to prevent friction against infant skin.

## **3.3 Manufacturing & Sourcing Model**

To maintain lean operations and adapt quickly to market demand, GROWIEE uses a **Subcontracted Cut-To-Make (CMT)** model with vetted domestic apparel factories across Sri Lanka.

* **Fabric Sourcing:** Direct procurement of export-surplus and custom-knit 100% combed cotton from top-tier Sri Lankan fabric mills.  
* **Quality Assurance:** Independent batch inspections for colorfastness, snap pull-strength, and seam alignment prior to final packaging.

# **4\. Financial Model & Unit Economics**

## **4.1 Production Cost Breakdown (Per 3-Kit Pack)**

By batching manufacturing into 3-piece sets, GROWIEE lowers per-unit cutting, sewing, and packaging costs.

* **Fabric & Trim Cost:** LKR 1,100 \- LKR 1,300  
* **CMT / Manufacturing Labor:** LKR 600 \- LKR 750  
* **Packaging & Branding Materials:** LKR 150 \- LKR 200  
* **Estimated COGS Per Pack:** **LKR 1,850 \- LKR 2,250**

## **4.2 Price & Margin Structure**

* **Target Retail Selling Price (RSP):** LKR 4,850 \- LKR 5,200 per 3-Kit Pack  
* **Gross Profit Margin:** \~55% \- 62%  
* **Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC \- Meta Ads):** LKR 800 \- LKR 1,000  
* **Fulfillment & Subsidized Delivery Offset:** LKR 200 \- LKR 300  
* **Net Operating Margin (EBITDA):** **\~25% \- 35%**

## **4.3 Recommended Production & Inventory Ratio**

To optimize stock turn and avoid over-investing in single sizes, manufacturing runs follow a **1 : 3 : 3 : 2** allocation weighting based on average customer repeat purchase cycles and gifting patterns:

| Size Category | Allocation Percentage | Strategic Rationale |
| :---- | :---- | :---- |
| **Newborn (NB)** | 12.5% (1 Share) | Fast outgrown; primarily purchased for hospital bags and immediate gifting. |
| **0–3 Months** | 37.5% (3 Shares) | Core entry point for new parents; highest immediate purchase rate. |
| **3–6 Months** | 37.5% (3 Shares) | Peak usage phase; strong repeat purchases from returning customers. |
| **6–9 Months** | 12.5% (2 Shares) | High retention segment as babies grow into active movement. |

# **5\. Fulfillment & Logistics Strategy**

## **5.1 Domestic Courier Partners**

GROWIEE partners with established 3PL logistics providers in Sri Lanka to ensure reliable, nationwide delivery:

* **Primary Logistics Partners:** Koombiyo Delivery, Domex Courier, and Pronto Lanka.  
* **Coverage Area:** 100% islandwide coverage across all 25 districts of Sri Lanka.

## **5.2 Delivery Cost Structure & COD Processing**

Cash-on-Delivery (COD) remains the primary payment method for online shoppers in Sri Lanka. GROWIEE's fulfillment model is structured to streamline these operations:

* **Flat-Rate Delivery Fee:** Standard LKR 450 – LKR 550 charged to the customer at checkout (or integrated into promotional "Free Shipping" tiers for multi-pack orders).  
* **COD Remittance Cycle:** Weekly courier remittances to support steady operational cash flow.  
* **Packaging:** Moisture-sealed, branded outer mailers that protect cotton garments from weather damage during transit.
